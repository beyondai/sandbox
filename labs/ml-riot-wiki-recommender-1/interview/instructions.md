# Interview instructions: MLE Deep Tech (Generalist), ML Project Review

Source: `notes/mle-interview-deep-tech-riot.pdf`, pages 1 and 4. The ML
design info is in `original-design-to-review.md`.

## Role and setting

- Your team lead asks you to review a junior engineer's project report.
- Come ready to discuss it.

## What to prepare

The report has strengths, weaknesses, and gaps. Some things are done well,
some are done poorly, and some important things are missing. Be ready to
discuss these 4 topics:

1. What was done well.
2. What you would change or fix, and why.
3. What is missing that should be there.
4. Questions you would ask the author.

## Rules

- You can use any tools to prepare.
- Do not use supplemental tools during the interview. "We want to know
  what you think."
- Prepare for less than 2 hours. Focus on what matters most.

## Format

- It is a working session, not an oral exam. Treat it like a review of a
  colleague's work, and discuss what you would change.
- The interviewer asks follow-up questions during the discussion, as in a
  real team discussion.
- Be ready to screen share. You may have to sketch or diagram your ideas.
- Total time is 1 hour. About 45 minutes is discussion, after intros and
  before your questions at the end.

```
 0 min        ~5                      ~50          60
 |-- intros --|-- discussion (~45) --|-- your Qs --|
```
