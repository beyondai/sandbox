At Riot, internal teams produce a lot of documentation — post-mortems, design
specs, onboarding guides, runbooks, and knowledge base articles. Over time, the
internal wiki has grown to tens of thousands of pages. Engineers frequently
report that they can't find what they need, and new hires spend significant time
searching for relevant docs during onboarding.  The ML engineering team has been
asked to build a recommendation system that suggests relevant articles to
employees based on what they're currently reading.
